# Live-Demo Runbook — REIT Target AI

**NMIMS B.Sc. Finance | Business Analytics | Theme 4 — Building Agents/Artifacts Using Generative AI**
**Author: Vishesh Jain. All data in this project is synthetic.**

A six-to-eight-minute demonstration sequence, three evaluator-chosen exercises
with the results the application should produce, and what to do if something
goes wrong. Expected figures are computed at full precision by
`data-pipeline/scripts/runbookExpected.js` with the application's own engine
(`AnalysisRun.compute()`) for dataset version 2.0.0, seed 20260919, and written to
`data-pipeline/generated/runbook_expected.json`; values are rounded only for
display. The test suite recomputes concentration, weighted yield, year-0 rent and
the 3-year value for exercises A, B and C by a separate route (T-178), and the
browser acceptance script drives the exercises through the page controls (EX-*).
A passing System Check shows that the engines work on the current data; it does
not by itself confirm every figure below or how each page looks. Each figure can
also be checked by hand with the formulas given.

## 2. Evaluator exercises

Formulas used below: gross yield = monthly rent × 12 ÷ value; HHI = Σ share²;
year-0 rent = existing rent + amount × target gross yield; year-0 value = existing
value + amount; base-case value after 3 years = year-0 value × 1.08³. Yields are
quoted to four decimals in the working (Gurugram — Cyber Hub 7.0275%, Ambattur
8.9035%) because multiplying a rounded 7.03% would not reproduce the page.

**The amount and the score.** The investment amount does not enter the scoring
formula: the five factors are properties of each segment, and the diversification
factor uses the portfolio's existing shares, not the amount. The amount changes
only the after-investment concentration, weighted yield and projections.

### Exercise A — the evaluator changes the investment amount and the weights

**Inputs (example).** Investment ₹75 Cr; then custom weights rental yield 35%,
rental growth 25%, diversification 20%, demand 10%, low market risk 10%.

**Steps.** Market Screener → type 75 in Investment Amount → Enter. Then move the
sliders to 35 / 25 / 20 / 10 / 10 (they move in steps of 5). While they do not
total 100% the total shows in red and nothing is applied; at 100% the preset
becomes Custom and every page updates.

**Expected behaviour and results.**

| Step | Shortlist candidate | Raw / eligible rank | Score | City HHI after | Asset-type HHI after | Year-0 rent | 3-year base value |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Balanced, ₹50 Cr (start) | Gurugram — Cyber Hub, Delhi NCR | 5 / 1 | 67.71 | 0.3496 | 0.6588 | ₹36.789 Cr | ₹692.84 Cr |
| Balanced, ₹75 Cr | Gurugram — Cyber Hub, Delhi NCR | 5 / 1 | 67.71 | 0.3293 | 0.6711 | ₹38.546 Cr | ₹724.33 Cr |
| Custom 35/25/20/10/10, ₹75 Cr | Gurugram — Cyber Hub, Delhi NCR | 6 / 1 | 64.64 | 0.3293 | 0.6711 | ₹38.546 Cr | ₹724.33 Cr |

- Changing the **amount** changes concentration and projections, not scores or ranks.
- Changing the **weights** re-ranks: GIFT City, Ahmedabad becomes the highest
  raw-score market (75.87) and fails the screen on both counts; the candidate
  keeps eligible rank 1 at raw rank 6.
- By hand: ₹75 Cr × 7.0275% = ₹5.2706 Cr added rent → 33.275 + 5.2706 = ₹38.5456 Cr,
  shown as ₹38.546 Cr. Year-0 value 500 + 75 = ₹575 Cr; × 1.08³ = ₹724.33 Cr.
  City shares after 75 Cr in Delhi NCR (total ₹575 Cr): Mumbai 295, Pune 90,
  Bengaluru 85, Hyderabad 30, Delhi NCR 75 → Σ share² = 0.3293.
- Agent Output: no stored commentary matches (the amount and weights differ);
  the page names the differing inputs. In live mode, **Run Agent Analysis** works
  for any configuration.

**Pages that must agree.** Market Screener candidate panel, Overview plate,
sidebar summary, Diversification (HHI, projections), Agent Output context table,
Decision Report.

**Likely viva questions.** Why did the ranking not change with the amount? (The
score does not use the amount.) Why does asset-type HHI rise? (More office.) Why
only eligible rank 1 at raw rank 6? (Five segments above it fail the screen.)

**Reset.** Press **Balanced** and set the amount to 50, or use Reset Demo.

**If something fails.** Weights not applying → the sliders move in steps of 5 and
nothing is applied until they total exactly 100%; the total shows in red with the
reason, and every page keeps the last valid analysis (the old design that
redistributed the other weights automatically was replaced). Amount ignored → it
must be a positive number up to ₹1,00,000 Cr; anything else is not applied and a
message under the field says why and which amount the analysis keeps. Any doubt →
Reset Demo and repeat.

### Exercise B — the evaluator provides a small custom portfolio

**Inputs (example).**

| Asset ID | Name | City | Locality | Type | Value (₹ Cr) | Rent (₹ Cr) | Area / occupied (sq ft) | Lease expiry | Tenant |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EX-1 | Exercise Office A | Mumbai | Andheri East | Commercial Office | 60 | 4.2 | 300000 / 285000 | 2030-03-31 | IT |
| EX-2 | Exercise Mall B | Pune | Kharadi | Retail | 30 | 2.1 | 150000 / 135000 | 2029-06-30 | Retail |
| EX-3 | Exercise Homes C | Bengaluru | Whitefield | Residential | 10 | 0.4 | 80000 / 72000 | 2028-12-31 | Residential |

**Steps.** Portfolio → **Custom Portfolio** → **Start Blank — Add First Asset** →
fill the form → **Add Asset**; **+ Add Asset** for the next two.

**Expected results (Balanced preset).**

| Measure | Expected |
|---|---|
| Portfolio value / rent / weighted yield | ₹100.00 Cr / ₹6.700 Cr / 6.70% |
| City HHI and asset-type HHI before | 0.4600 and 0.4600 (shares 0.6, 0.3, 0.1) |
| Default investment | ₹10.00 Cr (10% of the custom portfolio) |
| Shortlist candidate | Gurugram — Cyber Hub, Delhi NCR (raw rank 5, eligible rank 1) |
| City HHI after / asset-type HHI after | 0.3884 / 0.4876 |
| Weighted yield after | 6.73% |
| Year-0 rent | ₹7.403 Cr (6.700 + 10 × 7.03%) |

The ranking itself changes slightly, because the diversification factor depends
on the portfolio: Baner, Pune drops from raw rank 2 to 4 now that Pune is 30% of
holdings.

**If an amount was typed earlier, it is kept.** The default amount is 10% of the
active portfolio only while the user has not typed one. If the evaluator typed
₹75 Cr in Exercise A and did not reset, the custom portfolio keeps ₹75 Cr: city
HHI after 0.3339, asset-type HHI after 0.6278, year-0 rent ₹11.971 Cr. Reset Demo
(or setting the amount back) returns to the default.

**Pages that must agree.** Every page now uses the custom portfolio:
Diversification, Overview (Current analysis says "custom portfolio"), Agent
Output, Decision Report. Stored agent commentary no longer matches (the
portfolio is part of the scenario key).

**Likely viva questions.** Why did the default investment change? (It is 10% of
the active portfolio.) Why did a market's rank change when only the portfolio
changed? (Diversification benefit.) Why both HHI figures 0.46? (Same shares by
city and by type in this example.)

**Reset.** Portfolio → **Sample Portfolio** (the custom one is kept, inactive), or
Reset Demo. **Reset Custom Portfolio** deletes the custom holdings.

**If something fails.** The form shows the field and the reason (for example,
occupied area larger than total area); correct it and press **Add Asset** again.
Escape closes the form without changes.

### Exercise C — the evaluator asks for an alternative target

**Inputs (example).** Under Balanced, select Ambattur, Chennai (raw rank 1),
which fails the screen.

**Steps.** Market Screener → **Show all 50 markets** if needed → **Select** on
Ambattur's row. Then press **Income Focused** to show the manual choice is kept.

**Expected results.**

| Measure | Expected |
|---|---|
| Label on every page | "Manually selected target"; Select button reads "Selected (manual)" |
| Warning | The manual selection differs from the current shortlist candidate (Gurugram — Cyber Hub, raw rank 5, eligible rank 1) |
| Ambattur's support | 26 simulated observations, Assumption Support Grade D — fails both tests; external calibration Unverified |
| City HHI after / asset-type HHI after | 0.3496 / 0.6588 (Chennai is also a new city for the portfolio, and the target is office, so the same as Delhi NCR) |
| Weighted yield after | 6.86% (Ambattur's gross yield 8.90% is higher) |
| Year-0 rent | ₹37.727 Cr (33.275 + 50 × 8.9035% = 33.275 + 4.4517) |
| After pressing Income Focused | Ambattur stays selected (now raw rank 2); the warning names the new candidate, Aerocity, Delhi NCR |

**Consequences to explain.** The analysis now describes a segment that fails the
simulation-support screen: higher yield, but its figures rest on the minimum
number of simulated observations and an assumption set the project graded D.
The Decision Report labels the model's candidate and the manual target
separately. Stored commentary does not match (selection mode and target are in
the scenario key). **Return to automatic recommendation** restores the candidate.

**Likely viva questions.** Why allow a manual choice at all? (The committee
decides; the tool makes the consequence visible.) Why does the city HHI match
Delhi NCR's? (Same amount into a new city.) Why is a higher-yield, higher-scoring
segment not the candidate? (The screen; raw score measures attractiveness only.)

**Reset.** **Return to automatic recommendation**, then **Balanced**; or Reset Demo.

**If something fails.** If the page shows another target, check the sidebar for
the selection mode; Reset Demo always restores the canonical state.

## 3. Recovery

| Problem | Recovery |
|---|---|
| Live agents fail (quota, network, key) | The cards say "Fresh generation was unavailable" with the reason and show no AI text; the deterministic analysis, checks and report are unaffected. If needed, switch to the static tab and **Show Pre-generated Analysis** for a preset at its defaults — and say it is stored commentary that matches this exact configuration, not evidence of a live run. |
| A live reply fails the consistency check | It is sent back once with the specific problems. If the revision also fails, the card shows it as **Withheld** (text collapsed, not authoritative) and it is not passed to later agents or printed in the Decision Report. |
| A page looks inconsistent | Reset Demo; then repeat the step. The acceptance tests show every page renders the same run. |
| Browser cached an old version | Hard refresh (Cmd+Shift+R / Ctrl+Shift+R). |
| Proxy will not start | Check Node 18 or later; check `server/.env` exists with `GEMINI_API_KEY`; use the static site meanwhile. |
| Evaluator asks to load an entirely new market dataset | Not supported in the interface, and not one of the Theme 4 required outputs. The Data Centre CSV import demonstrates the cleaning pipeline only and never changes the segments (areas need an explicit unit: `areaSqFt`, `areaSqM`, or `area` with `areaUnit`). The reproducible route is the pipeline: write observations for the existing 50 segments in the observation schema, then run `deriveMarkets.js`, `computeStatistics.js`, `buildObservationDistribution.js` and `buildMeta.js` (`docs/data-documentation.md`, Section 9). |

## 1. Purpose

This document explains how market data was constructed, estimated, and simulated for the 50-segment Indian REIT investment target universe. All numbers are synthetic: each assumption was written to follow a cited benchmark or was derived / estimated with documented assumptions, and nothing is reverse-engineered from application outputs. None of the cited benchmark documents has been located or traced (docs/SOURCE_VERIFICATION_REPORT.md), so external calibration is Unverified for every segment.

## 3. Source Hierarchy

Sources are classified into five tiers by credibility:

| Tier | Description | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | REIT Annual Reports / SEBI Filings | Embassy REIT AR 2024, Mindspace AR 2024 |
| 2 | Regulator / National Data | RBI, SEBI, Census of India |
| 3 | Institutional Broker Research | JLL, Knight Frank, CBRE, Cushman & Wakefield, ANAROCK |
| 4 | Trade Association / Industry Body | NAR-India, CREDAI, PropTiger |
| 5 | Expert Judgment / Synthetic | Assumption register, model-generated |

Higher-tier sources always override lower-tier sources for the same metric. All 13 sources are recorded in `source_register.csv`.

---

## 4. Evidence Classification

Every estimate in `market_estimates_long.csv` carries one of five classifications:

| Classification | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Reported** | Directly quoted from a Tier-1 or Tier-2 source without transformation |
| **Derived** | Calculated from reported figures using a documented formula (e.g., gross yield = rent × 12 / price) |
| **Estimated** | Inferred from city-level benchmarks or comparable micro-markets with explicit adjustments |
| **Synthetic** | Generated by the triangular model where no real data exists; flagged for high uncertainty |
| **Missing** | Data not available; field is null in generated observations |

---

## 5. Assumption Support Grades

The grade records how each segment's ASSUMPTION SET was constructed and how wide a band was assumed. The "Interpretation" column states the kind of benchmark the assumptions were intended to follow; none of those benchmarks has been located, so no grade is evidence of real-market accuracy.

| Grade | Uncertainty Band | Interpretation (intended basis — unverified) |
|---|---|---|
| A | ±5–8% | Direct quotation from Tier-1 source; multiple confirming sources |
| B | ±10–12% | Good Tier-1 or Tier-2 coverage; minor interpolation |
| C | ±15–20% | Tier-3 broker research; city-level benchmark with locality adjustment |
| D | ±20–30% | Tier-4/5; estimated from comparables with significant uncertainty |
| E | ±30–40% | Largely synthetic; treat as scenario placeholder only |

## 6. Estimation Approach

### 6.1 Monthly Rent (INR/sqft/month)

1. For markets with direct REIT or broker data → classified **Reported** (Grade A or B)
2. For markets with city-level data → locality-class adjustment applied:
   - Premium: city benchmark × 1.15–1.30
   - Established: city benchmark × 0.90–1.10
   - Growth: city benchmark × 0.70–0.90
   - Peripheral: city benchmark × 0.55–0.75
3. For markets with no data → Grade E (synthetic), lower/upper = central ±35%

### 6.2 Sale Price (INR/sqft)

Derived from rent using a documented capitalisation multiplier based on Gross Cap Rate:

> sale_price = monthly_rent × 12 / cap_rate

Cap rate ranges taken from Tier-3 broker research. Classification is always **Derived**.

### 6.3 Gross Yield (%)

Always derived; never independently estimated:

> gross_yield = (monthly_rent × 12) / sale_price × 100

In the generated observations, `gross_yield_pct_derived` is computed from the sampled rent and price values to maintain internal consistency.

### 6.4 Rental Growth (% p.a.)

Anchored to:
- Embassy REIT / Mindspace REIT disclosed escalation clauses (8–15% annual contractual)
- JLL India Office Market reports for market-level growth

Adjusted downward for Retail and Residential markets per sector norms.

### 6.5 Demand Score & Risk Score

Drawn from their own triangular distributions, whose central estimates are computed from the documented formulas:

> demand_score = 0.40 × occupancy + 0.35 × (rental_growth / 0.15 × 100) + 0.25 × location_strength  
> risk_score   = 0.35 × vacancy_risk + 0.35 × supply_risk + 0.30 × liquidity_risk

Clamped to [0, 100]. Input factors sourced from broker market outlook reports.

## 7. Semi-Synthetic Observation Generation

### 7.1 Algorithm

Each of the 2 000 observations is drawn independently from a Triangular distribution:

```
Triangular(lower_estimate, central_estimate, upper_estimate)
```

using the **inverse-CDF method**:

```
Let u ~ Uniform(0, 1) from Mulberry32 PRNG (seed 20260919)
Let fc = (mode - lower) / (upper - lower)
If u < fc:  value = lower + sqrt(u × (upper - lower) × (mode - lower))
Else:       value = upper - sqrt((1-u) × (upper - lower) × (upper - mode))
```

### 7.2 PRNG

Algorithm: Mulberry32 (32-bit, fixed seed 20260919). Reproducible: identical seed always produces identical observations.

### 7.3 Metrics sampled independently

`monthly_rent_psf`, `sale_price_psf`, `rental_growth_pct`, `capital_growth_pct`, `vacancy_rate_pct`, `occupancy_rate_pct`, `demand_score`, `risk_score`, `transaction_volume_index`.

`gross_yield_pct_derived` is **always post-computed** from the sampled rent and price to prevent spurious inconsistency.

## 8. What This Pipeline Is Not

- It does **not** connect to or modify the Market Screener application.
- It does **not** replace or modify `public/data/markets.json` or `public/js/*.js`.
- It does **not** contact external APIs, Firebase, or Gemini.
- Generated files live only in `data-pipeline/generated/`.
- All estimates are for academic illustration; they should not be used for actual investment decisions.
